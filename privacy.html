Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Ontario Furnace Rebates ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit our quote form we collect the details you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address, service address, and a description of your request. We do not collect this information unless you choose to send it.

How we use it

We use your information solely to respond to your request, provide estimates, and arrange service. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Data retention

We keep request details only as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal or business record-keeping requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@ontariofurnacerebates.ca.
