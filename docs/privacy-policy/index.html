Privacy and project inquiries

The optional calculator and contact form collect the details you enter, including name, email, phone, postal code, heating source and project description. A submission also includes its page URL and marketing-source parameters when present.

Why the information is collected

Your details are used to review and follow up on a furnace and hybrid quote review inquiry. The site uses a Headbanger Marketing lead-processing workflow. A relevant service provider may receive the inquiry to respond to the work requested. A submission is not an official funding application.

Keep sensitive documents out of the form

Do not send identity numbers, income files, banking details, passwords or government-program account credentials here. If an official program needs evidence, submit it through that program's verified process.

Questions or changes

Use the contact form to ask about information you submitted or to request that follow-up stop. This notice describes the inquiry flow; a provider or official administrator may have a separate policy for its own services.
