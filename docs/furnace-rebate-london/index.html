Furnace planning in London: a diagnostic repair price alongside replacement

Before accepting a replacement recommendation, request the fault and repair price in writing. The condition of the heat exchanger, combustion components and other equipment should be assessed by the qualified responding provider.

A diagnostic repair price alongside replacement

Compare the repair with a gross furnace replacement price and, if appropriate, a designed heat pump option. A funding maximum should not make a more expensive project look unavoidable when the actual alternatives have not been explained.

  • Keep the diagnostic findings.
  • Compare warranties and exclusions.
  • Show anticipated rebates on separate lines.

Check the rebate line

A furnace-only replacement has no HRS heat pump rebate. If the proposal includes a qualifying heat pump, check its separate equipment, rate and mandatory pre-approval. The city does not create a special provincial furnace entitlement.
